# Own Your Payments

## Objective

Product-story video (20 to 35 seconds): a bolted-on payment gateway is not a payments strategy. Contrast Version A (kicked out to a clunky third-party portal) with Version B (payment stays inside the product), then close on ownership of the payments experience.

## Audience

Software platform leaders in Must-Pay industries: healthcare, K-12 education, childcare, senior care, and other essential services.

## Notes

Source: Jimmy's LinkedIn post "A bolted-on payment gateway is not a payments strategy" plus his storyboard: user clicks Pay, Version A ugly portal, freeze on "This is not embedded payments", cut to Version B in-product, flash card / ACH / cash / reconciliation / Merchant Hierarchy, end on "Your payment infrastructure should not set your product roadmap", final "Do you own your payments experience?"

Claims used: embedded payment flows; reconciliation and reporting; Merchant Hierarchy; card, ACH, and cash; unique isolated environment; PCI Level 1 compliance. All from the post; no invented numbers.

Stills are coded UI mockups (no real client names, no dollar amounts) rendered to videos/own-your-payments/stills/.
